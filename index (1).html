About CDF Studio

A manufacturing partner built around the needs of early-stage brands.

CDF Studio is a B2B clothing manufacturing partner for startup and early-stage fashion brands. We work with founders who are developing new collections and need a structured, dependable production process — not just a factory that processes an order.

From fabric sourcing and sampling through production, quality control, and shipment coordination, we manage each stage with consistent communication, realistic timelines, and a focus on fit, finish, and repeatable quality.

Mission

Make clothing manufacturing more accessible, reliable, and responsible for startup fashion brands.

Vision

Become a trusted long-term manufacturing partner for founders who value clarity, quality, and ethical production.

How We Work

A clear process from brief to delivery.

Each stage is structured so you know what's happening, what comes next, and what to expect — without having to ask.

01

Brief & Alignment

We review your designs, specifications, and timeline. Before anything moves forward, we confirm everything in writing.

02

Fabric & Sampling

Fabric sourcing followed by sample development. We work through feedback until the sample matches your standard.

03

Production & Quality Control

Garment production with quality checks throughout — not just a final inspection at the end.

04

Shipment & Handover

Coordinated delivery with full documentation. We debrief after each run so the next one is more efficient.
